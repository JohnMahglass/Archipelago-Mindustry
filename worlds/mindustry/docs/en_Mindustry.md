# Mindustry Archipelago

Mindustry is a game about building factories and defending them. This APWorld connects its campaigns to an Archipelago multiworld. Most research nodes become location checks; researching them reports their locations to the server, while received items unlock blocks and other progression. World options can also add sectors and resources as checks.

The randomizer supports the Serpulo and Erekir campaigns, either separately or together. The selected goal determines what is needed to complete the world.

Playing requires the modified [Mindustry Archipelago client](https://github.com/JohnMahglass/Mindustry-Archipelago-Randomizer/releases) and a compatible [Mindustry APWorld](https://github.com/JohnMahglass/Archipelago-Mindustry/releases). See the Mindustry Multiworld Setup Guide for installation, connection, and starting a fresh campaign.
